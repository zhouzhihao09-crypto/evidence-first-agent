# Tender Requirements Document
# This document lists all documents required for the tender submission.

## Required Documents

1. Valid insurance certificate (must be current and cover the tender period)
2. Tax compliance certificate (latest fiscal year)
3. Business license (current and valid)
4. Financial statements for the last 3 years
5. Audited financial report for the latest fiscal year
6. Evidence of past project experience (minimum 3 similar projects)
7. Quality management certification (ISO 9001 or equivalent)
8. Health and safety record (no major incidents in last 5 years)
9. Environmental compliance certificate
10. Surety bond or financial guarantee

## Submission Requirements

- All documents must be dated within the last 12 months
- Certified copies required for public documents
- Foreign documents must be notarized and translated
